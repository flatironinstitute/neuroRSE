Robert Wong is a Ph.D. candidate in Systems Science and Mathematics at Washington University in St. Louis. His research combines computational neuroscience and machine learning to study visual coding during development. Robert received his B.E. with Honors in Electrical and Computer Engineering and a Bachelor of Mathematics from The University of Queensland.
