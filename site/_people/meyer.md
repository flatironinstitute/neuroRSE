Emily is a PhD student in computational neuroscience at the University of Pennsylvania. She is interested in combining behavior, electrophysiology, and modeling in order to better understand visual object recognition abilities across species. She currently works with tree shrews as her model organism as a window into the evolutionary origins of primate vision. Prior to grad school, Emily received her B.S. from Tulane University and performed research as a postbac at NEI/NIH.
