Raffles is an MD-PhD student at McGill University. Combining computational and psychophysical approaches, he is currently investigating the neural basis of visual disorder amblyopia or lazy eye. His research journey began in a biophysics lab, where he helped optimize laser settings to visualize trajectories of diffusive nanoparticles, during which he realized the shared computational principles that enable both computers and humans to see. He's passionate about bridging basic vision science and clinical ophthalmology to develop new diagnostics and therapeutics for vision disorders.
